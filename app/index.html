Do not miss these

GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them away from your trophy save.

    Road to the platinum

    Finish the story first, then the side stories, then the one-off partner and robbery trophies, then the collectibles and the long grind, and finish with the 100% cleanup. Around 200 hours in total; the story alone is about 60.
